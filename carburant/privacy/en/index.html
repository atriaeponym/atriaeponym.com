What leaves your phone — Mon Carburant

2026-11-28

No account, no ads, no analytics tool. Here is, without exception, what the app sends, and to whom.

The price file

The app downloads the public price file from the State's website (donnees.roulez-eco.fr, or data.economie.gouv.fr as a backup). Like any website, it sees your phone's IP address. No location or search is sent to it: the file is the same for everyone.

Other countries

For each other country you tick, the app downloads the public price file from that country's official site (energia.serviciosmin.gob.es, www.mimit.gov.it, precoscombustiveis.dgeg.gov.pt). Like any site, it sees your phone's internet address. No location or search is sent to it.

Electric charging

If you ask for the charging stations, the app downloads the public files of tariffs, charge points and their availability from the French State's site (proxy.transport.data.gouv.fr). Like any site, it sees your phone's internet address. No position or search is sent to it.

The route

When you ask for a route, the start and destination are sent to the routing service of IGN (French State), which returns the path of the route.

When you search for an address or a place for a route, what you type is sent to the address service of IGN (French State), which suggests addresses in France.

The map

When you display the map, the map background comes from Google (Android) / Apple (iPhone): the area you are looking at is therefore known to it, along with your phone's IP address.

Alerts

To check the prices of your favourites, the phone reads the public price file again from time to time, as it does when the app is opened. Notifications are created on the phone: no server knows your stations or your thresholds.

Your location

It is used to work out distances on your phone. The app sends it to no one and does not store it. On the map, the provider of the background knows the area shown, and so where you are when the map is centred on you.

The only exception: if you take your position as the start or end of a route, it is sent to the IGN route service, like any other address.

The ‘Get directions’ button

The station's location is passed to your phone's navigation app, which applies its own rules.

The rating window

When you come back to the app after ‘Get directions’, the Google Play or App Store rating window may appear, at most once every 120 days. It belongs to the store: the app knows neither whether you rated nor what you wrote. So as not to ask for it too often, the phone keeps the number of these returns and the date of the last request.

The free trial

The date of your first launch is stored on this phone to count the 14 days of trial. It is not sent anywhere.

The subscription

The purchase is made in Google Play (Android) / App Store (iPhone), which knows about it and applies its own rules. The app only asks it whether a subscription is running. Atria.Eponym receives neither your name nor your means of payment.

What stays on your phone

Your choices, your favourite stations and the latest price file. To delete them, uninstall the app.
